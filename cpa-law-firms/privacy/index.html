Privacy

Last updated November 26, 2026.

What we collect

When you use the contact form on the CPA and law firm pages, we collect the details you type: your name, firm, work email, optional phone, firm type, approximate number of clients and any notes. Cloudflare may process technical data such as your country to deliver the page and the form.

How we use it

Only to reply to your request, set up a call, send you the agreement if you decide to start, and arrange the work. We do not sell this information and we do not share it with advertisers.

Where it goes

Form submissions are emailed to we@weautomatetheworld.com and recorded in a spreadsheet that we use to track requests, using Google Workspace tools.

Client data

The form is not for client or taxpayer information. Please do not send any through it. Work with client information happens only after a signed data processing and confidentiality agreement.

Your choices

Email we@weautomatetheworld.com to ask us to correct or delete what you sent, or to stop contacting you.
