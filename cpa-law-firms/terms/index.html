Money-back guarantee and terms

Draft, last updated November 26, 2026.

What the $600 Plug-In Sprint covers

WATW connects to the files and accounts for one case you choose and gets the agents running on that case. You receive a work pack with sources and a written plan. We aim to deliver within three business days of receiving your access [to be confirmed]. The Sprint does not include ongoing services.

The guarantee

If after your review you see no result, or you do not think our proposal is good, tell us and we refund the full $600 right away. You do not need to give a reason beyond that.

How to request a refund

Email we@weautomatetheworld.com within [7] days after your review of the work pack. No form and no reason are needed. We issue the refund immediately, the same day we receive your request [to be confirmed]. The card network then typically takes about 5 to 10 business days to show the refund on your statement; that part is outside our control.

Confidentiality and data handling

Before we access any client information we sign a confidentiality agreement and a data processing agreement with your firm. Work happens inside your firm's own accounts, access is revocable at any time, and on request we delete or return any copy of your data within [30] days. If you request a refund, the confidentiality terms continue to apply.

Not professional advice

WATW is not a CPA firm or a law firm and does not give tax, accounting or legal advice. A licensed professional at your firm reviews and signs all work. For law firms the system is a software tool licensed for the firm's use. We do not refer clients or share fees.

After the Sprint

Recurring services start only under a separate written agreement that you sign. Nothing recurs automatically from the $600 start.
