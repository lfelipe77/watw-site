How the agents work

Five agents in sequence. One person signs.

The agent never rewrites a plan. It finds the files that deserve attention, shows why, and lines up what the professional needs to review them.

  1. 01
    Collector

    Reads the client folder index and the documents you authorize: wills, trust agreements, gift tax returns, Form 706 history, beneficiary designations.

  2. 02
    Calculator

    Extracts dates, parties, exemption references, formula clauses and funding facts into one structured record per client, and compares the client's balance sheet to the current federal amounts.

  3. 03
    Checker

    Matches every extracted item to the page it came from. Anything it cannot source is left blank instead of guessed.

  4. 04
    Risk reviewer

    Flags files with older review dates, terms tied to the exemption amount, portability steps that are not documented, and clients with assets but no plan.

  5. 05
    Re-checker

    Re-reads the flagged files a second way and drops any flag it cannot reproduce.

  6. ✍
    You

    The attorney or CPA reviews the queue, decides which clients to call and signs off.

The basis

The rule, in plain English

The number

For decedents dying and gifts made in 2026, the basic exclusion amount is $15,000,000, up from $13,990,000 for 2025 (IRS, Rev. Proc. 2025-32). The generation-skipping transfer exemption for 2026 is also $15,000,000.

Permanent, and indexed

Section 70106 of P.L. 119-21 amended Section 2010(c)(3) to remove the scheduled reduction. The amount is adjusted for inflation for 2027 and later years. Congress can still change the law in the future.

Annual gifts

The annual exclusion per recipient stays at $19,000 for 2026. For gifts to a spouse who is not a U.S. citizen it is $194,000.

Rate

The top rate for estate, gift and generation-skipping transfers is 40% on amounts above the exclusion (Form 709 instructions).

Portability

A surviving spouse can add a predeceased spouse's unused exclusion (DSUE) only if the predeceased spouse's estate made a timely and complete election on Form 706.

State taxes

Some states impose their own estate or inheritance tax with different thresholds. The agent flags each client's state so the professional can check it.

Questions

Common questions

What is the federal estate tax exemption in 2026?

The basic exclusion amount is $15,000,000 per person for decedents dying and gifts made in 2026, according to the IRS. Married couples can use two exclusions, and portability can add a deceased spouse's unused amount when the election was made.

Does the agent give estate planning advice?

No. It organizes information and flags files for review. Advice and plan changes stay with the licensed attorney or CPA.

Does this replace our document management system?

No. The agent reads from the folders you already use and writes its review queue back to you.

Which clients does it flag?

The criteria are set by the firm. Common examples are older review dates, terms that reference the exemption amount, undocumented portability and assets with no plan on file.
