How the agents work

Five agents in sequence. One person signs.

The same five agents run overnight, every night.

  1. 01
    Collector

    Reads the official feeds and pages you choose: federal, and the states where your clients operate.

  2. 02
    Calculator

    Matches each item to client profiles (industry, entity type, states, size, tools used) using criteria you set.

  3. 03
    Checker

    Confirms every summary line against the source text and attaches the link. Items it cannot source do not go in the digest.

  4. 04
    Risk reviewer

    Marks effective dates, comment deadlines and items that supersede earlier guidance, such as a delayed or replaced law.

  5. 05
    Re-checker

    Re-reads each flagged item a second way and drops any match it cannot reproduce.

  6. ✍
    You

    A partner or attorney reads the digest, decides which clients to contact and what to say.

The basis

What it watches

Federal

Federal Register notices and proposed rules, Congress.gov bill and law text, IRS newsroom and Internal Revenue Bulletin, Treasury releases.

State

Legislature bill status and enacted text, attorney general and agency rulemaking pages, state revenue department notices.

Dated items

Effective dates, compliance deadlines and comment periods are pulled into the digest so they do not get lost in summaries.

Changes to earlier changes

Laws get delayed and replaced. In 2025 Colorado delayed its AI Act to June 30, 2026, and in May 2026 replaced it with a law effective January 1, 2027. The monitor tracks the replacement, not just the first version.

Source link first

Every item carries the link to the primary text. If the monitor cannot find one, the item is held back.

Your call

The monitor reports what changed and who may be affected. It does not interpret the law for a client.

Questions

Common questions

Which sources does the monitor use?

Official sources: the Federal Register, Congress.gov, IRS and Treasury releases, and state legislature, agency and revenue department pages for the states you choose. Every digest item links to its source.

How does it know which clients are affected?

It matches changes to the client profile fields you provide, such as industry, entity type and states. The match is a suggestion for a professional to review.

Does the digest give legal or tax advice?

No. It reports what changed and where to read it. Interpretation stays with your attorneys and CPAs.

How often does it run?

Daily, with a short email or message each morning.
