How the agents work

Five agents in sequence. One person signs.

Each client goes through the same five agents. Nobody skips a step, and every flag points back to a ledger line.

  1. 01
    Collector

    Pulls the vendor ledger or payment export from the client's accounting system, plus the W-9s already on file, and lists what is missing.

  2. 02
    Calculator

    Totals payments by vendor and payment type for the calendar year and compares each total to the threshold in effect, using the rule written as code.

  3. 03
    Checker

    Matches every total back to ledger lines and every vendor name to its tax ID record. Mismatches come back marked.

  4. 04
    Risk reviewer

    Flags what usually causes notices: missing taxpayer IDs, vendors split across two names, payments near the threshold, payment channels that change who reports.

  5. 05
    Re-checker

    Re-runs the totals a second way and releases the list only if both results match.

  6. ✍
    You

    The CPA reviews the list, decides who gets a 1099 and signs off.

The basis

The rule, in plain English

What changed

Section 70433 of P.L. 119-21 raised the Section 6041(a) reporting threshold from $600 to $2,000 for payments made after December 31, 2025. Section 6041A (services) and the backup withholding threshold in Section 3406 follow the same dollar amount.

Indexing

For calendar years after 2026 the $2,000 base amount is adjusted for inflation and rounded to the nearest $100 (new Section 6041(h)). The agent reads the threshold in effect for each payment year instead of hard-coding one number.

What did not change

Royalties and broker payments in lieu of dividends still use the $10 floor and direct sales of consumer products still use $5,000. Backup withholding that was actually withheld must be reported whatever the amount (IRS Pub. 1099).

Still being finalized

Treasury published a proposed rule (REG-113229-25) on April 17, 2026 to conform the regulations to the new threshold. Proposed rules can change before they are final, so the agent tracks the final text.

E-filing

The e-file requirement applies when a filer has 10 or more information returns in total, counting all types together (T.D. 9972, effective for returns filed on or after January 1, 2024).

Your call

Whether a given vendor is reportable depends on entity type, payment type and facts only the professional can confirm. The agent organizes the evidence; it does not decide.

Questions

Common questions

What is the Form 1099 threshold for 2026?

For payments made after December 31, 2025, the threshold for most Form 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC reporting is $2,000 in a calendar year, up from $600. It is indexed for inflation starting with calendar years after 2026 (IRS Publication 1099).

Does the agent decide who gets a 1099?

No. It totals payments, checks W-9 status and flags exceptions. A CPA at your firm reviews the list and decides.

Does it work with our accounting software?

It works from exports such as a vendor ledger or payment report. We confirm the format on the first day, so you do not have to change systems.

Is the threshold the same in every state?

The $2,000 figure is the federal threshold. State information-return rules can differ, so the agent flags the client's state for your review.
