Your firm runs on documents, deadlines and judgment. The documents and the deadlines never stop. This is the machine that carries them. We plug your firm in, and the work starts moving.

Rules change. Clients multiply. And every change starts the same hunt: which clients does this touch, and what do we need from each of them? Your team does that by hand, again and again.

So we flip it. We plug your firm into our machine. It connects to where your work already lives: your document folders, your practice software, your inbox. It runs under your own login and your own brand, and every client file stays under a signed confidentiality agreement. Nothing to rebuild.

Inside the machine, five agents work in a line. The collector gathers the documents and finds what is missing. The calculator runs the numbers by the rule. The checker matches every figure back to its source. The risk reviewer looks for what usually causes problems. And the re-checker repeats the work a second way, and releases it only when both answers agree.

Point it at one job at a time. Sorting vendors against reporting thresholds. Reviewing estate plans after a law changes. Running deduction scenarios across a client book. Watching sales tax exposure by state. Or reading client contracts against new privacy and AI rules. Same machine, different job.

And it never stops at one client. The machine sweeps the whole book, every client against every rule. A watcher reads federal and state changes every day and matches each one to the clients it touches. You get a clear list: who is affected, why, and what is next.

Nothing leaves without a professional. Every step is written to a log that cannot be erased. You review it, you correct it, you sign it. The machine prepares. You decide.
